# Font sources

Downloaded directly from DaFont on 25 October 2026.

- Butler by Fabian De Smet: https://www.dafont.com/butler.font
  Download: https://dl.dafont.com/dl/?f=butler
  DaFont identifies it as 100% free for personal and professional projects.
  The original EULA is included as Butler-License.pdf. Regular, bold, and black WOFF2 files are shipped.
- Gidole by Andreas Larsen: https://www.dafont.com/gidole.font
  Download: https://dl.dafont.com/dl/?f=gidole
  DaFont identifies it as 100% free and open source. The original archive contains Gidole-Regular.ttf.
  Project: https://github.com/larsenwork/Gidole

Fonts are self-hosted. No external font requests are made at runtime.
